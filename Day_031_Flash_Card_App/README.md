# Day 31 - Flash Card App

## 📌 Project Overview
A Flash Card application to learn French vocabulary. The app shows a French word for 3 seconds, then automatically flips to show its English translation.

If you know the word, click ✅ and it will be removed from your learning list. If you don't know it, click ❌ to try again later.

## 🎯 What I Learned
- Tkinter Canvas and Images
- `window.after()` and `window.after_cancel()` for timers
- Pandas - Reading CSV and converting to Dictionary
- Exception Handling - `try / except / else`
- Data Persistence - Saving progress in `words_to_learn.csv`
